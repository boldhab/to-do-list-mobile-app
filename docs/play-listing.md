**Name** — Task Planner  
**Category** — Productivity  
**Contact** — [publisher support email]

**Short description**

Plan goals, tasks and recurring reminders offline.

**Full description**

Break monthly goals into weekly objectives and daily tasks. Use the calendar to see what is scheduled, move tasks as plans change, and track completion across your planning periods. Keep independent tasks alongside linked work, repeat routines on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule, and set optional reminders.

Find saved work through search and filters. See completion trends with charts based on your own tasks. Your planner is stored on your Android device and can be used offline. Reminder delivery depends on Android settings.

**Release notes**

First release. Plan goals and tasks offline, schedule recurring work and optional reminders, and review progress with a calendar and productivity charts.

## Submission checklist

- Confirm the owner-controlled package ID, Play App Signing and upload certificate.
- Supply approved launcher artwork, a feature graphic, and phone screenshots from the actual release build. The bundled launcher is temporary development artwork.
- Publish the completed privacy policy at a public HTTPS URL and inject that URL into the app at release build time. Link it in Play Console and in Settings. [Review requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455?hl=en)
- Complete Data safety after reviewing the final signed bundle and all included SDKs. The current release configuration is local-only and removes internet permission. Confirm app access, ads, target audience, content rating, supported countries, device support and any account-specific declarations in Play Console. [Data safety requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)
- Qualifying personal accounts created after November 13, 2023 require at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for 14 days before applying for production access. Verify which track and requirements apply to the publisher account. [Closed testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en)
- Obtain the owner’s approval before any upload, publication or rollout.
