# Task Planner privacy policy

Draft for **[developer or publisher name]**. Effective date **[publication date]**. Privacy contact **[monitored email or contact page]**.

Task Planner stores the tasks, descriptions, goals, dates, completion history, recurring schedules, reminder settings and preferences you enter. It uses this information to show your planner, calculate productivity statistics on your device, and schedule optional reminders through Android.

The Android release configuration stores this information in the app’s private storage. It excludes the app’s files and settings from Android automatic backup and device transfer. The app does not include an account service, advertising SDK, remote analytics service, or cloud synchronization. The release manifest removes internet permission. Android receives generic notification text, reminder timing and an opaque task identifier so it can schedule the reminder and open the task when tapped. Android controls notification visibility and delivery. Opening the published privacy policy uses your browser.

Your records remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete records from the planner or clear all app data through Android app settings. Uninstalling removes locally stored planner data under the app’s backup policy. The developer cannot recover your local planner records.

Use the privacy contact above for questions about this policy.
